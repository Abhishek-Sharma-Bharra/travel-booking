# Demo guide and three-minute presentation

Choose **Explore Demo** on the home page. No account, real customer data, payment provider or paid AI service is required. The gold banner identifies an isolated read-only agency. The sample spans six completed months relative to its first seed; use that date range for the fullest view.

Working interactions: date/source/destination/salesperson filters, enquiry search and stage filtering, list/pipeline views, enquiry details and activity history, CSV export, validated CSV preview, quotation print preview, monthly report PDF via browser print, and an in-memory model calculator. Forms can be explored; saving explains the demo is read-only. Signup creates an entirely separate empty agency.

## Script

| Time | Show | Suggested words |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:40 | Overview and a destination filter | “This is a fictional agency. We connect enquiries, bookings and collections so your team can review its work in one place. Contribution is before advertising and operating expenses, not net profit.” |
| 0:40–1:20 | Enquiries, pipeline, an open record | “Every enquiry has an owner, a follow-up and a history. We can search or import records with validation. This probability is a synthetic demonstration model; it has not been validated on your business.” |
| 1:20–2:00 | Quotation preview and Bookings | “Your package and enquiry become a printable quotation, then a booking. Partial receipts and supplier expenses are separate. We record transactions manually; we do not move money.” |
| 2:00–2:40 | Marketing and Monthly report | “These channels use explicit first-touch source attribution. This report shows collections, customer balances and package contribution, with practical review actions. It can be printed or saved as PDF.” |
| 2:40–3:00 | Settings checklist | “We can onboard your team, packages and enquiries into your own private workspace. Subscription and onboarding terms are managed manually. The first step is your owner's account and agency settings.” |

Do not present the demo's synthetic model metrics, illustrative sales or contribution as real customer results. There are no testimonials, fabricated client logos or claimed savings. Explain current-cohort reporting rather than implying the report is a closed-period accounting statement.

## A separate write-through demonstration

Create your own disposable agency, add one package, add an enquiry with current/future travel dates, build a quotation and accept it. Record a partial receipt, an actual supplier expense and a supplier payment. Show the remaining balances. To demonstrate cancellation, set the retained fee and record any refund and supplier credit explicitly. Use only fictional contact information for demos.
