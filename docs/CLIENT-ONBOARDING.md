# First paid pilot: operator checklist and offer

Sell the workflow you can demonstrate: enquiry assignment/follow-up, quotation to booking, manual collections/supplier balances, transparent contribution and monthly review. Do not sell guaranteed revenue, real-world ML accuracy, payment processing, tax filing or audited net profit.

## Before taking real client data

Complete PRODUCTION-RELEASE.md: actual email delivery, verification/recovery, operator MFA, least-privilege runtime DB, paid/persistent hosting suitable for the commitment, live backup/restore evidence, independent access review and reviewed service/privacy terms. Demonstrations can continue with fictional data. A custom website domain is optional; verify your real sender and hosting/email compatibility.

## Suggested offer to test, not a market-validated price

Starter: ₹1,499/month per agency, 3 seats and 3,000 stored enquiries. One-time onboarding suggestion: ₹3,000 for profile setup, up to 10 packages, one validated import up to 200 enquiries and one team-training session. Agree applicable taxes with your accountant. Extra migration work, custom changes and extra training need a separate written quote. Growth/Professional limits exist in the app, but set their prices only after measuring your hosting/support costs.

Subscription fees are collected/invoiced outside TravelOS. A service operator records Active access and an explicit validity period in `/admin`. Inactive clients retain view/export access; business writes stop. Never tell a client that clicking Save subscription charged a card. Trial access lasts 14 days and uses fictional data until launch prerequisites are met.

## Onboard one agency

1. Agree a written order and a named client owner. Create the owner's account with their email; they choose a private password and verify it.
2. Owner completes profile/contact/footer, sets up packages, then invites team members by email. Each recipient uses the invited address, chooses their own password and accepts the one-use link. No shared passwords.
3. Import a small authorised sample CSV, inspect errors, confirm assignees/dates/prices. Do not import passports, identity documents or unnecessary sensitive data.
4. Reconcile a real sample only after approval: quotation subtotal/discount/tax, accepted booking, partial receipt, supplier expense/payment, cancellation/refund/credit. Compare against source records with the client, not just the app's own totals.
5. Confirm source attribution and KPI definitions. Train on follow-ups and exports. Retain the “Demo model — not validated on your agency” label.
6. Agree support hours, incident contact, retention/export/deletion process and actual backup/recovery commitments. Test a disable/revoke operation with a disposable member.
7. Review after the first week. Collect concrete feedback and support-time costs before selling annual plans or larger deployments.

## Order template to complete and review

- Provider legal name/address and client legal name/address: **fill in**.
- Named administrator, support email and agreed hours: **fill in**.
- Plan, seats/record limit, subscription amount/taxes, billing date and validity: **fill in**.
- Onboarding deliverables, dates and client responsibilities: **fill in**.
- Hosting region, subprocessors and authorised data scope: **confirm actual configuration**.
- Cancellation/refund/export/deletion and backup retention/recovery terms: **agree supported terms**.
- Privacy/data-processing responsibilities, liability and applicable law: **obtain appropriate legal review**.

These are commercial drafting prompts, not a completed contract or legal certification. The provider's legal identity, policies and promised service levels cannot be invented by the application.
